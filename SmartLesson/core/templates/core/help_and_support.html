Getting Started

  • Teachers can create, edit, and submit CBC-aligned lesson plans under the “My Lesson Plans” section.
  • Administrators can review, approve, or reject submitted plans and give feedback.
  • ICT Officers manage user accounts, assign roles, and ensure system functionality.
  • Access your dashboard to view lesson plan statistics and progress summaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I submit my lesson plan for approval?

Go to My Lesson Plans, click “Edit” or “Create”, fill out your lesson details, and click Submit for Review. You'll receive a notification once it's reviewed.

2. Can I edit a lesson plan after it's approved?

No. Approved plans are locked to preserve the approval record. However, you can duplicate and modify it as a new lesson plan if needed.

3. I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click the “Forgot Password?” link on the login page to reset your password via your registered email address.

4. I'm not receiving notifications.

Ensure your notifications are enabled and check the “Notifications” page under your profile. Notifications appear when your lesson plan is reviewed or when admins send updates.
