Perfect — here’s a comprehensive yet clear explanation of all user roles in your SmartLesson Planner: A Digital System for Competency-Based Lesson Planning and Supervision in Kenyan Schools.

The roles are:
1️⃣ Teacher
2️⃣ Administrator (HOD or Principal)
3️⃣ ICT Officer

Each plays a distinct but connected role in the digital lesson planning ecosystem.
Below is a detailed explanation of each — their responsibilities, key functions, and how they interact within the system.

1. Teacher Module
    Overview

    The Teacher is the primary user of the SmartLesson Planner.
    This role focuses on creating, managing, and submitting CBC-aligned lesson plans for review and approval.
    It replaces the traditional handwritten lesson books used in Kenyan schools.

    Core Responsibilities

    Digital Lesson Creation:
    Teachers prepare lesson plans directly in the system using structured digital forms aligned to the CBC format (learning outcomes, activities, assessment, etc.).

    Editing and Updating:
    They can edit their lesson plans anytime and update them as teaching progresses.

    Lesson Plan Storage:
    Plans are automatically stored in the system database, reducing the risk of data loss and ensuring accessibility anywhere.

    Plan Sharing:
    Teachers can share lesson plans with colleagues for collaboration or mentorship.

    Notifications:
    Receive system alerts about feedback from administrators or plan approval/rejection.

    Tracking Feedback:
    View comments from HODs or admins for continuous improvement.

    Key Features (from your UI):

    Create Lesson Plan → Start a new CBC lesson plan.

    My Lesson Plans → View all personal lesson plans and their approval status.

    Shared Plans → Access plans shared by other teachers for reference.

    Notifications → Alerts for feedback, updates, and system notices.

    Value to Teachers

    ✅ Saves time and paperwork
    ✅ Ensures CBC compliance
    ✅ Encourages collaboration
    ✅ Makes lesson review fast and transparent

2. Administrator (HOD / Principal) Module
    Overview

    The Administrator role represents Heads of Department (HODs), Deputy Principals, or Principals responsible for supervising and monitoring teaching activities.
    They ensure that all submitted lesson plans meet quality standards and are implemented effectively.

    Core Responsibilities

    Review and Approval:
    Review teachers’ submitted lesson plans to ensure alignment with CBC requirements and approve or reject with comments.

    Supervision:
    Monitor which teachers have submitted plans and track those pending or overdue.

    Coverage Reports:
    Generate reports showing subject or class coverage, progress, and areas needing attention.

    Manage Teachers:
    Add, edit, or deactivate teacher accounts where necessary.

    Provide Feedback:
    Comment on lesson plans to guide teachers toward improvement.

    Key Features (from your UI):

    Review Lesson Plans → Access submitted plans for review.

    Approve / Reject Plans → Provide feedback and manage plan approval.

    Coverage Reports → View statistics and summaries of teaching progress.

    Manage Teachers → Control teacher accounts, roles, and permissions.

    Value to Administrators

    ✅ Simplifies supervision
    ✅ Enhances accountability and transparency
    ✅ Reduces paperwork for tracking progress
    ✅ Provides instant access to reports and records

3. ICT Officer Module
    Overview

    The ICT Officer manages the technical and operational aspects of the SmartLesson Planner.
    They ensure the system runs smoothly, securely, and is accessible to all users.
    This role is particularly relevant in schools implementing digital platforms for the first time.

    Core Responsibilities

    User Management:
    Create, edit, and deactivate user accounts (teachers and admins). Assign appropriate roles and permissions.

    System Maintenance:
    Conduct regular system updates, manage backups, and ensure data security.

    Activity Monitoring:
    Track user logins, plan submissions, and activity logs for accountability.

    Support and Troubleshooting:
    Help users resolve login, access, or technical issues.

    Backup Management:
    Schedule regular system backups to prevent data loss.

    Key Features (from your UI):

    User Management → Add, update, or delete users and assign roles.

    System Backups → Create and restore system backups for data safety.

    Activity Logs → View all system operations and user activity.

    Value to ICT Officers

    ✅ Ensures system reliability and uptime
    ✅ Provides data integrity and backups
    ✅ Enhances user security and privacy
    ✅ Offers clear audit trails of all actions
